# Add an obstacle sensor

Finish assembly steps **8–11**. The yellow front beam holds the ultrasonic distance sensor. Its round eyes point forward. Plug the sensor into **D**.

Use **Locate a device** in Assembly to highlight the sensor and its hub port. In the port monitor, move a large flat object in front of the sensor and watch the distance change. Objects that are tiny or angled may not reflect the sound well.

Mark complete after checking the mounting, cable and measurement.
